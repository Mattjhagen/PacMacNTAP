# Hologram Partnership Call Script

**Meeting Date:** September 14, 2026 @ 3:00 PM CDT  
**Contact:** Kieran Sanders (BDR)  
**Duration:** 30 minutes

---

## Opening (2 min)

**Kieran:** Hi Matt, thanks for connecting. Tell me about PacMac Mobile.

**You:** 
"Hey Kieran, thanks for making time. So PacMac Mobile is a wireless service provider - we currently serve consumer customers with phone plans, but we're expanding into IoT connectivity. 

We're getting a lot of demand from business customers who need to connect things beyond phones - vehicle trackers, security cameras, point-of-sale terminals, Raspberry Pi projects, that kind of stuff.

We've already built the customer-facing platform and admin dashboard. We're basically integration-ready - we just need the right partner on the connectivity side. That's why I'm reaching out to Hologram."

---

## Discovery (5 min)

**Kieran:** What kind of volumes are you looking at?

**You:**
"So we're thinking pilot with 10 to 50 devices in the first month, just to validate everything. But based on our customer pipeline, we'd scale to 200-500 devices within six months, and we're targeting 1,000+ by end of year one.

The use cases are pretty diverse - GPS trackers that send small data bursts, tablets in retail kiosks with moderate usage, some security cameras that'll need more bandwidth. So we need flexibility on the data side."

**Kieran:** What coverage areas?

**You:**
"Primary focus is US - pretty much nationwide coverage. We've got customers in urban and rural areas, so good coverage outside cities matters. International would be a future expansion, but US-first."

---

## Partnership Structure (8 min)

**You:**
"So I want to understand how your reseller program works. We're looking to white-label this - PacMac owns the customer relationship, does all the support and billing, and we're buying the connectivity from you wholesale. What does that partnership look like on your end?"

*[Let him explain their program]*

**Follow-up questions:**
- "What's the pricing structure - is it a per-device fee plus per-GB, or how do you structure it?"
- "Are there any setup fees or monthly minimums we'd need to hit?"
- "What's typical markup that resellers work with?" *[This helps you understand their expectation]*
- "Is there a formal reseller agreement, and how long does that approval process take?"

---

## Technical Requirements (7 min)

**You:**
"On the technical side, we've already built API integration into our platform. What's the process to get API credentials and start testing?"

*[Let him explain]*

**Follow-up questions:**

**You:**

## Pricing Discussion (Critical - don't leave without this)

**You:**

Like, what would wholesale pricing look like for, say, 100 devices with average 1GB per month usage?"

## Key Phrases to Use

✅ **"We're integration-ready"** - Shows you're serious  
✅ **"We own the customer relationship"** - Clarifies partnership model  
✅ **"We need flexibility"** - Sets expectation for various use cases  
✅ **"We're not starting from scratch"** - Differentiates you from tire-kickers  
✅ **"What's the ballpark?"** - Gets pricing without formal commitment  

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

🚩 Won't give any pricing indication  
🚩 High minimums (like $5K/month) before you've tested  
🚩 Long contract (>6 months) required for pilot  
🚩 Vague on API access timeline  
🚩 Can't provide test SIMs at reasonable cost
